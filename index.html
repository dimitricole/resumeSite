Ovenlight

Your own web apps, in your pocket.

You or your coding agent built a web app that runs on your own computer. Ovenlight opens it on your iPhone behind Face ID, and for anyone you invite, without putting it on the internet. The connector on your computer is also an MCP server, so your agent can publish the app, check it and read its logs.

A Home Screen of words, not icons.

Whengo

Weather scored to your own limits.

Whengo is a weather tracker for people sensitive to heat, poor air and sun. It scores every hour of the week ahead against your own limits, then shows the hours that fit.

  • Feels-like, air quality and UV
  • Hourly, seven days ahead
  • Free, no ads
